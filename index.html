🌊 Welcome to

Narwhal Station

More Than Music — It's a Station for You!

Narwhal Station is a fun, safe, and educational web app made for the students of SST Northwest (San Antonio, TX).

It combines music, games, and learning to help you relax, stay curious, and have fun while building important skills.

💗 Why This App?

The goal of Narwhal Station is to:

  • 🎵 Give students a positive, school-safe way to enjoy music
  • 🎮 Make learning fun through interactive games
  • 🧠 Support creativity, focus, and relaxation
  • 👥 Bring our school community together
⭐ Because school isn't just about work — it's also about joy, creativity, and discovering what you love. 💙
